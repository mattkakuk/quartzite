polished for the home.

Unlike man-made materials, every quartzite slab has its own personality. Some
slabs are soft and subtle, with creamy whites and gentle gray lines. Others

Many homeowners compare **granite and quartzite** before making a final

Granite usually has a more speckled or granular pattern, while quartzite often
has flowing movement and veining. Granite is a classic choice, while quartzite
is often selected by homeowners who want something more elegant and marble-
like.

## **How to Care for Quartzite Countertops**

Quartzite is strong, but like all natural stone, it benefits from proper care.
The good news? Maintenance does not have to be complicated.

### **Seal the Surface**

Quartzite should be sealed to help protect it from stains and moisture.
Sealing creates an added layer of protection and helps preserve the stone’s
appearance.

### **Clean With Mild Products**

For daily cleaning, mild soap and water are usually enough. We recommend
avoiding harsh chemicals, abrasive pads, and acidic cleaners.

### **Use Simple Everyday Habits**

Use cutting boards, wipe spills quickly, and place trivets under hot cookware.
These habits are small, but they help keep the stone looking its best.
