## **Maintenance Tips for Quartzite Countertops**

Quartzite is durable, but like all natural stone, it benefits from proper
care. With simple habits, homeowners can keep their countertops looking
beautiful for years.

### **Clean Spills Promptly**

Daily cleaning is simple. Use a soft cloth, mild soap, and water for routine
cleanup. Wiping spills quickly helps maintain the surface and keeps the
kitchen looking fresh.

### **Use Cutting Boards and Trivets**

Even though quartzite is strong, we still recommend using cutting boards and
trivets. These small habits protect the surface and help preserve its finish.

### **Seal When Needed**

Natural stone may require sealing depending on the specific slab and finish.
Sealing helps protect the stone and supports long-term performance.
